# Code of Conduct

Open Life Alliance exists to make room for every choice. We expect the same spirit in our
community spaces: this repository, issues, discussions, chats and events.

## Our standards

We expect everyone to:

- Be kind and patient, especially with newcomers and people writing in a second language.
- Respect people who live differently — including people who smoke or drink. We oppose
  *pressure*, not people.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Assume good intent, and ask before assuming.

We do not tolerate:

- Harassment, intimidation, stalking or unwelcome attention.
- Discrimination or hate speech based on any personal characteristic, including ethnicity,
  nationality, religion, gender, sexual orientation, disability, age or health status.
- Shaming people for their habits, health or recovery journey.
- Spam, advertising, or using the project to promote products or services.
- False claims — for example presenting membership as a medical, legal, religious or
  government certification, or claiming to speak officially for the alliance without authority.
- Publishing others' private information without their consent.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove content and restrict or ban participants who break this code.
Report problems to the maintainers by opening a private security advisory on GitHub or, once
published, through the project's contact address. Reports are handled confidentially.

Consequences scale with severity: a private note, a public warning, a temporary ban, or a
permanent ban.

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces and when someone represents the project in public.
