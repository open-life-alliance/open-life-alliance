# Name and logo guidelines

The code is MIT-licensed and the content is CC BY 4.0, but the **Open Life Alliance name, the
logo and the official card designs** identify the project. These guidelines keep them
trustworthy.

## You may, without asking

- Use the name and logo to refer to the Alliance — in articles, posts, talks, school projects.
- Share your own membership card anywhere.
- Use the logo files in `public/logo/` unmodified, with enough clear space around them.
- Say that your project is "built with" or "inspired by" Open Life Alliance code.

## Please do not

- Imply that you, your company or your product speaks for, is endorsed by, or is an official
  chapter of the Alliance unless the maintainers have agreed in writing.
- Modify the logo (colours other than the official color/white/black/on-dark variants,
  proportions, added elements), or combine it with another logo.
- Use the name or logo on products for sale, or to collect money or donations.
- Present a card or certificate as a government, medical, religious or legal certification.
- Run a fork of this website under the Open Life Alliance name. Forks are welcome — please
  give them a different name and logo.

Questions? Open an issue on GitHub.
