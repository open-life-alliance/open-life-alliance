# Open Life Alliance — Charter

*Version 0.1 · draft for community discussion*

## 1. Name

Open Life Alliance ("the Alliance"). In Simplified Chinese: 健康自主倡议.
Motto: **Your life. Your choice.** — *Live free. Live well.*

## 2. Nature

The Alliance is an open, voluntary, community-driven initiative. It is not a religion, a
political party, a medical service, or — at this time — a registered legal entity, charity or
international organization. Any change to its legal status will be announced publicly.

## 3. Mission

To support every person's right to choose how they live, free from social or professional
pressure to smoke, drink or adopt unhealthy habits — and to promote respect for everyone's
choices.

## 4. Principles

1. **Smoke-free living** — no one should be pressured to smoke.
2. **Alcohol-free living** — celebration, friendship and business do not require alcohol.
3. **Personal freedom** — joining and leaving are free; no one's life is checked or scored.
4. **Respect for everyone** — we oppose pressure, not people.

## 5. Membership

- Joining is free. Membership never depends on payment, donations or purchases. Any paid
  extras the Alliance may offer later (for example special badge designs or merchandise) are
  optional and are never a condition for joining.
- Anyone may join with a display name of their choosing; real names are not required.
- Joining expresses support for the principles. It does **not** claim that a member has quit,
  or will never smoke or drink.
- Members may leave at any time, for any reason, without explanation.
- Membership cards are personal expressions of commitment, not certifications of any kind,
  and confer no legal exemption.

## 6. Privacy

The Alliance collects the minimum data needed, does not sell personal information, and keeps
personal health information private by default.

## 7. Openness

The website's code is open source (MIT) and its written content is shared under CC BY 4.0.
Decisions about the project are discussed in public on GitHub.

## 8. Money

The Alliance does not currently accept donations or sell anything. It may later welcome
voluntary donations or offer optional paid extras, such as special badge designs and
merchandise, to fund the website and outreach. Before any money is accepted, the Alliance will
publish who receives the funds, how they are used, its refund policy, and the relevant tax
information, and it will then report income and spending openly.

## 9. Changes to this charter

Changes are proposed as pull requests to this file and discussed openly before being merged
by the maintainers.
